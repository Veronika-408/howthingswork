# Contributing to howthings.work

Thank you for building a machine. Start with [docs/ADD-A-MACHINE.md](docs/ADD-A-MACHINE.md), or hand it
to your agent (it's also at https://howthings.work/contribute.md).

## Contributor Terms

By opening a pull request you agree to these terms for everything in it (pictures, prompts, texts, data and code):

1. **Credit.** Your machine is credited to the name and link you give in `subject.json`, on the site and
   anywhere the maintainer publishes it, for as long as it is used.
2. **Rights.** You grant Peter Hraska (the maintainer) a perpetual, worldwide, irrevocable, royalty-free,
   non-exclusive licence to use, copy, change, publish, distribute, sublicense and sell your contribution,
   in howthings.work and in anything made from it (for example books, apps, prints or courses), under any
   licence, including commercial ones. You keep your own rights to your work.
3. **It's yours to give.** You made the contribution, or have the right to submit it. It contains no one
   else's protected work (no logos, brands, copied images or text) and nothing harmful for children.
4. **No payment.** Contributions are voluntary and unpaid; the credit is the thanks.

Leave the box ticked in the pull request template to confirm.
