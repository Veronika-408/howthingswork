Image 1 is a finished pen-and-ink line drawing for a classic 1980s children's "how things work" encyclopedia.
Colour it with loose, transparent traditional watercolour washes, the classic line-and-wash look: soft flat washes of warm natural colour laid over the drawing, gentle granulation, soft uneven edges that now and then stray a little past the lines, lighter and darker passages within each wash, white paper left showing in the highlights. The washes are light and airy so every line stays clearly readable.
KEEP EVERY LINE EXACTLY AS IT IS: do not move, redraw, thicken, add or remove any line or hatching; do not change the composition, the framing or the size of anything. The paper around the subject stays plain white.
ABSOLUTELY NO TEXT: no letters, words, numbers or labels.

COLOURS: the top layer soft pinkish beige with the small cells in pale lilac and tiny purple nuclei; the bigger cut-open cell in lilac with a purple nucleus, orange-red mitochondria, lavender ribbons and a golden stack; the top surface pale peach; the middle layer warm rosy pink fibres; the hair and its pocket warm brown; the oil gland and the coiled sweat gland pale yellow, the sweat droplet clear pale blue; the nerves and touch sensors golden yellow; arteries red and veins blue; the fat cells golden yellow.
