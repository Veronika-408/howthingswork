Image 1 is a finished pen-and-ink line drawing for a classic 1980s children's "how things work" encyclopedia.
Colour it with loose, transparent traditional watercolour washes, the classic line-and-wash look: soft flat washes of warm natural colour laid over the drawing, gentle granulation, soft uneven edges that now and then stray a little past the lines, lighter and darker passages within each wash, white paper left showing in the highlights. KEEP EVERY LINE EXACTLY AS IT IS: do not move, redraw, thicken, add or remove any line or hatching; do not change the composition, the framing or the size of anything. The paper around the subject stays plain white.
ABSOLUTELY NO TEXT: no letters, words, numbers or labels.
